# v0.3 — Executable models and inference control

v0.2 established how this repository reasons. v0.3 turns that architecture
into an **executable** one: models now derive and test claims about plasmid
invasion, copy-number trade-offs and gene location, and the verification tools
state their own scope and demonstrate that their gates can fail.

The boundary worth naming is not "more material". It is that a computation in
this repository can now **change what the lecture should say**, and twice has.

Included:
- three model families, each with a committed check that re-derives its result
  rather than restating it: `models/invasion-threshold/`,
  `models/copy-number-burden/`, `models/gene-location/`
- the feasibility result: an optimum that exists in the algebra may be
  unreachable in the biology, and for the two commonest fixed-origin classes
  the copy-number trade-off does not exist at all — they are pushed in
  opposite directions
- the gene-location model, in which two published positions are shown to be
  corners of one inequality, the earlier recovered exactly as the `q → 1`
  limit of the later
- a committed bibliography-key checker and a claim-ID checker, each with a
  mutation test beside it that is required to go red
- the participant-firewall checker, with the `CHECKED` / `CHECKABLE` /
  `ATTESTED` vocabulary and a machine-enforced count of which clauses carry
  which mark
- adversarial review as a committed artifact: four reviews in `reviews/`, with
  dispositions tracked in `open-findings.md`
- the measurement-scope discipline: counts over analyst-chosen grids are
  labelled as such and never rendered as percentages of anything biological
- the instrument rule, in `CONTRIBUTING.md`: a passing check without a
  demonstrated failing control is weak evidence of coverage, recursively
  including the harness that runs the checks

Closed since v0.2, each deferred by name in that release's notes: the
invasion-threshold redesign (`OF-1`), the bibliography-key checker (`OF-2`),
the checked-or-attested marking of the participant policy (`OF-3`), and
model/notebook infrastructure. v0.2 said those were absent; they are present.

Explicitly deferred. Named items, not categories. Specific known defects with
drafted corrections remain individually listed in
[`../open-findings.md`](../open-findings.md).

- `OF-4` — the `CONTESTED` status is defined and carried by no content page.
  Deliberate: a candidate has now been identified (the size–PCN scaling law,
  disputed in the literature) but no content page yet makes the claim
- `OF-14`, `OF-15` — assurance debt in `models/gene-location/`: a nesting test
  that compares against a transcription rather than importing the file it
  names, and two controls that are algebraic consequences of one written line.
  Neither is a wrong result; both are weaker than their wording
- the scaling-law content itself, and with it the first real use of
  `CONTESTED`
- the OXA protein-evolution and *Shewanella* natural-history expansion
- empirical gene-location context from sequence databases
- broader case-study coverage beyond the single flagship
- machine-readable knowledge indexes / MCP
- release attestations, SBOM or an archival DOI, unless this becomes a
  citable research output

A v0.3 tag means the models and the checks are usable and internally
coherent, and that their scope is stated. It does not mean the science is
broad.

## What this release learned about its own instruments

Recorded because the repository's subject is the gap between a check and the
confidence placed in it, and v0.3 closed that gap by walking into it
repeatedly.

- **A verification harness reported `exit 0` for every script because it read
  `printf`'s status rather than the checker's.** A false "all checks pass"
  reached a commit message and a pull request before the next merge exposed
  it. The rule in `CONTRIBUTING.md` and the `sys.exit(3)` step both come from
  this.
- **A mutation case hardcoded a number that lived in another file.** When the
  number moved, the case raised instead of running — and because a raise
  aborts the run, ten other cases silently stopped executing. The test was not
  failing; it was not testing.
- **An absence claim was published in the same changeset that added the rule
  "an absence claim is a claim".** Its first stated cause was also wrong. Two
  defences were needed, not one: a result set truncated to the first *N* is
  never evidence of absence, and then the variant axes — whitespace, case,
  British against American spelling — must be defeated separately.
- **Two sessions sharing one working tree produced five stale reports and one
  commit on the wrong branch**, and the first symptom was never lost work but
  a confident false statement to the user. Discipline between sessions is an
  attestation; separate worktrees are a check. The repository's own vocabulary
  applies to the process that builds it.

Each of these was found by the next layer up rather than by the layer that
failed, which is the argument for having one.
